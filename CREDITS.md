# Credits and resources

**0.2.1 Arena Revolution:** original Blender architecture, shared crowd geometry and LOD tiers, original shader effects, arena ambience/chimes, loading and introduction presentation. Twelve fictional sponsor logos and the upright TR app icon were drawn with original vector/CoreGraphics source; the new icon uses no image-generation model. Existing cast assets receive movement and animation continuity improvements.


**Total Rush:** original stylized characters, rigs, signature wordmark, app icons, team emblems, arena, interface and synthesized sound effects.

**Original fictional sponsors:** Voltverb, Turbochoir, Sixth Sole, Rallyfract, Strideprompt, Tempostitch, Zonefern and Bassbracket. Symbols and wordmarks were designed for Total Rush.

**0.2.0 cast and animation:** forty human, animal and fantasy athletes, articulated rigs, UVs, palette, expressions, three detail levels and 37 clips per team family, authored in Blender. The Vector Core ball and its materials are original. No new Meshy generation or Nintendo animation is included in this version. Earlier experimental characters and animation are retired from the active assets.

**0.2.0 portraits:** forty original inked illustrations generated with the integrated image generation tool, following the cast briefs, then normalized for comic cut-ins and the portrait library; nameplate portraits were removed in 0.2.1. No Nintendo artwork was copied.

**Fonts:** Barlow by Jeremy Tribby / Barlow Project; Liberation Sans. SIL Open Font License. Licence texts are included in the game.

**Engine:** Unity 6.6, Universal Render Pipeline, Input System, Cinemachine, uGUI and TextMeshPro, under their respective licences.

Personal music and artwork are not distributed. No Nintendo assets or characters are included. This repository contains compiled game packages and player documentation, without Unity or Blender project sources.
